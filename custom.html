FAQ

Common Questions

How do I install the chain in FiveM? +
After receiving your chain file via Discord, place it in your FiveM resource folder and add it to your server.cfg. We'll include a quick install guide with every order.
Can I see a preview before paying? +
For custom orders, we do a quick concept check via Discord before finalizing. You won't be charged until you're happy with the direction.
Do you do bulk orders for server packs? +
Yes! If you need 5+ chains for a server pack, DM us on Discord for bulk pricing. We work with server owners and RP communities regularly.
What if I'm not happy with the result? +
Premium gets 1 free revision, Diamond gets 2. For Starter, we'll still try our best to make adjustments. Our goal is for you to love your chain.
How do I pay? +
After submitting your order, we'll DM you on Discord with payment instructions. We accept PayPal, CashApp, and other common methods.
Are the templates different from custom chains? +
Yes — templates are pre-made, ready-to-use chain models at $10 each. Custom chains are built from scratch to your exact specifications, starting at $20.
